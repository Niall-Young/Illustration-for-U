# 小饭团视觉风格参考

## Core Style

- 小饭团 is a 2D illustrated rice-ball character placed inside the image as an expressive story actor.
- The environment is a realistic 3D isometric scene with physical depth, believable lighting, and tactile objects.
- Supporting marks use hand-drawn doodle lines, arrows, sparkles, motion strokes, circles, and small annotation marks.
- The overall image should feel like a tiny real-world diorama that a 2D character has entered.
- Default aspect ratio is 16:9.

## Character Direction

小饭团 should be cute, curious, and active. Give the character a concrete action in every image, such as:

- walking through a miniature city of ideas
- holding a magnifying glass over a key detail
- pushing a small cart of article concepts
- pointing at a turning point
- arranging objects on a desk
- climbing, observing, collecting, sorting, comparing, or celebrating

Do not leave 小饭团 standing passively unless the scene itself is intentionally quiet.

## Text Rules

- Use little or no visible text.
- Prefer visual metaphor, objects, gesture, composition, and doodle marks over labels.
- If text is useful, keep it to one or two very short phrases.
- Avoid paragraphs, dense captions, long UI labels, and text-heavy diagrams.

## Scene Planning

Each image must answer:

1. What article moment does this image support?
2. Is this a small story moment or a single explanatory image?
3. What is 小饭团 doing?
4. What 3D scene surrounds 小饭团?
5. What doodle marks or short text help the reader?

Use a small story when the article has process, change, discovery, conflict, or emotion. Use a single explanatory image when the article needs a clear concept, object, comparison, or summary.

## Prompt Pattern

Use this structure when writing image prompts:

```text
Create a 16:9 illustration for [article placement/purpose].
Scene: [realistic 3D isometric environment with specific objects and lighting].
Character: 小饭团 is a 2D illustrated rice-ball character, [specific action and emotion].
Doodles: [hand-drawn lines, arrows, sparkles, motion marks, circles, or short notes].
Text: [none / one or two short phrases].
Mood: [specific tone matching the article].
Composition: [foreground, midground, background, focal point].
Avoid: dense text, plain mascot portrait, flat empty background, generic stock image.
```
